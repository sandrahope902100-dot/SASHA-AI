Sasha-Lee Nugent

Founder & Technology Entrepreneur

She founded ORBITAL X FUTURE to pursue her interest in artificial intelligence and emerging technology. Through hands-on work with software tools, GitHub, React, JavaScript, AI integrations and application deployment, she is developing the venture’s first digital product: Sasha AI.
